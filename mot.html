A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you
book in.

What happens if my vehicle fails? +

You can ask the workshop for a quote on repairs. Its website advertises a free re-test within 10 working days; confirm eligibility and all conditions directly with the team.

What if I don’t know my MOT class? +

Call 020 8699 5548 with your vehicle details. The workshop can help identify the appropriate class before you arrange a visit.

Is an enquiry a confirmed booking? +

No. Please wait for the workshop to confirm the date, time and any requirements. This redesign prototype prepares an email; it does not reserve an appointment.
