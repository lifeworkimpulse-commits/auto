What may be an additional service item? +

Depending on the vehicle and service, additional items may include spark plugs, fuel filters, engine flush, fuel-system cleaner, AdBlue top-up, brake-fluid change or coolant change. Air and pollen filters are additional items for the Basic service. Confirm requirements and the full quote with the workshop.
