ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

Your information
stays in your hands.

This is an independent redesign concept, not the garage’s live website. This static prototype has no enquiry database, payment processing or analytics. Form details are used in your browser to prepare an email and are not submitted to a server.

If you choose “Open email app”, your mail software handles the message. The design-direction preference is saved locally in this browser. Links to maps, GOV.UK and the original garage site open external services with their own policies.

A live launch requires the garage’s approval, confirmation of all content, appropriate privacy information and a tested enquiry-delivery setup.
